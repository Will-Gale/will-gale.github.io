About

Built for financial products that have to hold up in the real world.

Sonder Development works with founders, operators, and financial firms building products where product quality, technical depth, and execution discipline all matter. We stay close to the work and design for the operational realities that come with financial software.

We partner with post-raise founders needing execution, experienced operators launching a new fintech, and established financial companies modernizing existing platforms. The common thread is ambition paired with real product and operational complexity.

We help shape the product, make key technical decisions, and build with a high level of ownership. Instead of separating strategy from delivery, we keep both in the same conversation so momentum does not come at the expense of quality.

In fintech, the margin for vague thinking is small. We build for the demands that come with real money movement, regulated workflows, internal controls, and user trust, so the product stands up not just in demos, but in production.
